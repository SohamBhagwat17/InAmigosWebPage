Who We Are

A Foundation Built on Compassion & Unity

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation stands as a beacon of hope across India — mobilising volunteers, resources, and corporate partners to build a more inclusive and compassionate society.

  • Addressing education gaps caused by poverty in underserved communities
  • Championing women's rights, safety, and financial independence
  • Protecting wildlife and promoting a greener, sustainable environment
  • Running India's largest NGO internship program — Project Vikas
  • Operating across Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Maharashtra, UP, MP, and 28 states
Our Work

Six Projects, One Mission

Each project targets a unique social need — together they create holistic, lasting change.

🤲
Project SEVA

Distributing food, clothing, and essential supplies to the underprivileged — because no one should go to sleep hungry or cold.

🟢 Food drives · Clothing distribution · Shelter support
🏫
Project Bachpanshala

Ensuring quality education for underprivileged children through free learning centres, stationery drives, and mentorship programs.

🟡 Free education · Mentorship · School kits
👩‍💼
Project Udaan

Empowering women through skill development workshops, livelihood training, and awareness programs that foster financial independence.

🔴 Skill training · Women-led livelihoods · Awareness
🌱
Project Prakriti

Planting saplings, raising eco-consciousness, and driving environmental sustainability efforts to protect our planet for future generations.

🟢 Tree plantation · Eco drives · Clean-up campaigns
🐕
Project Jeev

Rescuing, feeding, and protecting animals — because compassion has no species boundary. Advocating for animal rights and welfare.

🟡 Animal rescue · Feeding drives · Advocacy
💡
Project Vikas

India's largest NGO internship initiative — training 30,000+ youth in digital, professional, and leadership skills to boost employability.

🔵 30K+ interns trained · Digital skills · Career growth

Social Impact

Why Our Work Matters

India faces complex, interlinked social challenges — poverty, illiteracy, gender inequality, environmental degradation, and animal cruelty. InAmigos Foundation attacks these root causes simultaneously with a people-first, community-led approach.

"In a world that often feels divided, InAmigos stands as a hope — dedicated to unity and support within society. We believe in the power of collective action to create a more inclusive, compassionate and empowered India."

— Govind Shukla, Founder & CEO
